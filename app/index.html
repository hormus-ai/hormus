03

Agents that keep watching.

Create an agent, tell it what to look for and let it run every hour. Give it skills from the community library like rug-check or whale-tracker, or write your own. Every report lands in your Activity with its sources, and any trade it suggests waits for your approval.

04

Alerts on Telegram.

Set a price alert on any token, or a screener that pings you when a new launch hits your numbers for liquidity, volume and buyers. Connect @hormusalerts_bot with one tap on the Alerts page and every alert lands in your Telegram, in the app and as a browser notification. Each one links straight to the token page.
